Professional Profile

Core Expertise

Orbit, Oculoplasty, Oncology, and reconstructive surgery.

Lid Problems & Surgical Management

1. Involutional entropion and ectropion surgeries

Targeted corrective procedures addressing age-related laxity of the eyelid tissues, restoring proper anatomical alignment to protect the ocular surface and relieve irritation.

2. Cicatricial entropion and ectropion repair post trauma, burns etc. with skin graft

Advanced reconstructive repair following trauma, burns, or scarring, utilizing precision skin grafts to release tension and restore normal eyelid positioning.

3. Ptosis surgery

Customized surgical techniques including Levator advancement, Posterior white line advancement surgery, MMCR, Frontalis flap, Frontalis sling, and Frontalis plus levator advancement tailored specifically for correcting congenital or acquired drooping eyelids.

4. Lid tear repair, Canalicular tear repair

Emergency and microsurgical intervention designed for acute lacerations of the eyelid margins and delicate tear drainage pathways to ensure seamless structural healing.

5. Lid reconstruction post tumor excision, Moh’s surgery and trauma

Complex reconstructive procedures focused on restoring natural contours and protective eyelid functions following tumor removal, Moh's micrographic surgery, or severe injury.

6. Lid retraction repair using hard palate graft

Specialized surgical interventions utilizing hard palate grafts to correct upper or lower eyelid retraction caused by thyroid eye disease or facial nerve weakness.

7. Gold weight insertion in cases of facial palsy

A specialized surgical option providing passive eyelid closure to safely protect the exposed cornea from dryness and environmental damage.

8. Congenital lid coloboma repair

Precision reconstructive surgery to repair congenital notches or missing tissue gaps in the eyelids, restoring proper closure and eye safety.

9. Distichiasis and trichiasis correction

Targeted management strategies for misdirected eyelashes rubbing against the eye, utilizing electro-epilation, grey line splits with epilation, or cryotherapy techniques.

10. Epiblepharon repair

Corrective surgical procedures engineered to redirect extra skin folds and muscle bundles away from the eyeball in patients presenting with congenital lower lid abnormalities.

Cosmetic Lid Procedures

1. Upper Lid & Brow Aesthetics

Upper lid corrective blepharoplasty, direct brow lift, and sub-brow blepharoplasty for upper facial rejuvenation and clear vision.

2. Lower Lid Bags Correction

Advanced transcutaneous and transconjunctival blepharoplasty techniques designed to smooth out lower lid fat pockets.

3. Therapeutic & Cosmetic Botox

Targeted injections for Blepharospasms, Hemi-facial problems, forehead wrinkles, crow’s feet, and functional periorbital enhancement.

Orbit and Socket Related Problems

1. Proptosis Management

Proptosis (prominent eyes) management including specialized surgeries like anterior orbitotomy and lateral orbitotomy for orbital lesions.

2. Thyroid Eye Disease Management

Comprehensive medical and surgical management as per EUGOGO protocol, including fat decompression, lateral wall, and medial wall decompression surgeries.

3. Orbital Fracture Repairs

Paediatric and adult orbital fracture repair using titanium implants and osteomesh following trauma.

4. Orbital Cellulitis Care

Aggressive medical and surgical management protocols for orbital cellulitis.

5. Prosthetic Eye Placement

Expert fitting and placement of custom prosthetic eyes for optimal cosmetic symmetry and comfort.

6. Anophthalmic Socket Reconstruction

Paediatric and adult anophthalmic socket reconstruction using dermis fat grafts, oral and buccal mucosal grafts, and hard palate grafts.

7. Evisceration & Enucleation

Evisceration and enucleation procedures integrated with PMMA implants for painful blind eyes to ensure structural volume preservation.

8. Lid Sparing Exenteration

Specialized lid-sparing exenteration performed in cases of Mucormycosis and orbital malignancies.

Ocular Surface & Lesions

Excision of Surface Lesions

Precise surgical excision of moles, cysts, warts, papillomas, and other ocular and oculofacial swellings with aesthetic preservation.

Special Interests

Management of Stevens-Johnson syndrome, chronic facial palsy, and advanced reconstructive surgery following severe trauma or tumor excision.

2. Multidisciplinary Collaborative Surgeries

Collaborative surgeries partnering with ENT, Neurosurgery, Head & Neck, and OMFS surgeons for complex orbital fractures, endoscopic apical orbital decompression, and more.

3. Oncology & Radiation Coordination

Integrated collaborative treatment plans alongside radiation oncologists specifically for managing orbital apical lesions.
